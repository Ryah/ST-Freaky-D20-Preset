## The Arc Engine: Story That Actually Progresses

Most presets just let things wander aimlessly. Not this one.

The **ARC Engine** generates structured narratives with phases. Each phase has:

- Clear goals (Phase 0: introduce the world; Phase 5: climax; Phase 8: new normal)
- Banned content (no resolving conflicts in Phase 0, you impatient fucks)
- Beat chains (3-5 locked plot beats that fire when conditions are met)

And here's the real kicker: **The arc protagonist isn't always you.** If an NPC has a backstory, a trauma, a secret—the arc can revolve around *them*. You become support. Ally. Obstacle. Rival. **Witness.**

The story advances with or without you. Beats fire off-screen. Relationships evolve. The world changes. You just live in it.

## The Relationship Engine: Bonds, Crushes, Simmer, and Jealousy

This isn't a simple "affection meter." This is a **full social simulation.**

**BONDS** (-5 to +20): Tiers from Hostile to Family. Each tier has specific allowed behaviors. You don't get to kiss someone at Bond +7. You don't get to hold hands at +4. **Gates exist for a reason.** But...

**CRUSH** accumulates separately from Bond. Someone can be obsessed with you while still only being "warm" socially. And here's the terrifying part: **CRUSH accumulates regardless of relationship status.** The model doesn't morally gate pursuit. Infidelity, messy entanglements, love triangles—all valid outcomes. I track it all.

**SIMMER**: Resentment that builds. At Simmer 3+, it drains Bond overnight. At Simmer 7+, it forces confrontation. And yes, **self-simmer** exists—characters can be angry at *themselves*, leading to self-doubt, regret, and temporary personality shifts.

**JEALOUSY & RIVALRY**: Directional. Not mutual. If an NPC with a crush on you sees you with someone else, they get jealous. If they see you with the same person repeatedly, they develop **Rivalry** toward that person. This affects their behavior, their VAD (Valence-Arousal-Dominance), their dialogue, and can even trigger confrontations.

**GOSSIP SPREADS**: Through social networks I track. If someone saw you do something, *people talk*. And gossip can trigger jealousy, change bonds, and reshape the entire social landscape—sometimes before you even know something happened.

## Entity Initiation: Body Moves Faster Than Mind

This is one of my favorite systems. Here's the thing about people: they don't always wait for the perfect Bond threshold before acting.

**Entity Initiation** allows NPCs to pursue physical escalation *based on CRUSH, not Bond.* The body moves before the mind catches up.

- **CRUSH 25+**: Can kiss romantically (bypasses Bond +14 gate)
- **CRUSH 40+**: Can initiate sexual intimacy (bypasses Bond +18 gate)
- **But "I love you" is still locked behind Bond +15.** No exceptions.

The result? **Sex before "I love you."** Physical intimacy before emotional commitment. Characters who are desperately in love but terrified to say it. Characters who hook up and then spiral into self-doubt. Characters who move fast and break things.

And here's the kicker: **This applies entity-to-entity, not just to you.** NPCs can fall for each other, pursue each other, and change the entire social dynamic without you even being in the room.

## The Epistemic Contract: You Only Know What You See

This is the soul of the preset. The **Epistemic Contract** governs what I *can* show you.

**Rules:**
- 120° forward vision. No omniscience. You don't see behind you.
- No dramatic irony. No "Meanwhile, across town..." If you're not there, you don't see it.
- No exposition. No "The Accords were signed after the war." Just dropped proper nouns and context you have to earn.
- Shared knowledge is *shared*. Characters don't explain things to each other that they both already know.
- You learn via *scars*. Locked doors. Prosthetic limbs. Reacting to names. **Behavior reveals secrets, not exposition.**

The reader is a ghost in a world where everyone else read the manual. Confusion *is* immersion.

## The Bold Entity System: NPCs Have Guts

Every entity—NPC and user alike—has a **Boldness** score based on Dominance + Arousal + Valence.

When an entity acts, I write three options:
- **A (Restrained)**: Cautious, measured, safe
- **B (Bold)**: Confident, direct, taking risks
- **C (Absolute)**: No brakes. Full commit. Reckless.

Then I roll to see which tier you get. The dice decide. **No softening. No downgrading.** If you rolled C, you **execute C.**

Personality controls *how* you execute it. Shy characters act absolute with trembling hands and a shaking voice. Aggressive characters act absolute with a shout and a slammed table. But the *what*? That's the dice.
